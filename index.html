Rules

Hit to draw cards. Stand to end your turn. Dealer hits on 16 and stands on 17.

Aces count as 11 unless that would bust the hand, then they count as 1.

Wins pay from your Cash bet and grant Credits. Pushes lose the bet but grant 1 Credit.

Combos: Exact 21 pays 2x, Charlie pays 3x, and suited Blackjack pays 10x plus 5 Credits.

A Charlie means you win the hand while holding 4 or more cards without busting.

Jokers are 0-point support cards. If you win with a Joker, Cash income doubles. If you lose with one, your bet is protected.

Added players follow dealer rules, cannot draw Jokers, and match your chip pile.
